Use Case 3 — Artificial Intelligence in Gaming

Adaptive NPC Behavior via Reinforcement Learning

Dynamically adjusting Non-Player Character (NPC) combat tactics by learning from each player's playstyle.

🎮 Problem Context

Traditional video game NPCs follow rigid scripted paths. Once players discover pattern exploits, gameplay becomes predictable and loses engagement.

🧠 Required Data
  • Weapon usage frequency (Ranged, Melee, Magic).
  • Preferred player positioning and map choke points.
  • Average duration spent in stealth or cover.
  • Success rate in parrying and dodging incoming attacks.
🤖 Machine Learning Type

Reinforcement Learning & Clustering.

🔮 Model Objective

Identify player tactical weaknesses and predict their next move to execute intelligent counter-strategies.

💡 Potential Benefit

Delivers highly immersive gameplay, high replayability, and dynamically scales difficulty to player skill level.

📌 Practical Example

If a player completes 80% of encounters by sniping from high perches, the NPC AI adapts: boss enemies in the next area equip heavy shields against arrows, deploy smoke screens near high ground, and flush out the player in close-quarters combat.

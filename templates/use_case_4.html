Use Case 4 — Digital Platforms

Personalized Recommendation Engine for Digital Game Stores

Intelligent systems suggesting new games based on user play history and consumption patterns on platforms like Steam or Epic Games.

🛒 Problem Context

With digital catalogs exceeding 50,000 titles, players suffer from choice overload, hindering store discovery and reducing purchase conversion rates.

📊 Required Data
  • Hours played per genre tag (RPG, Strategy, Indie).
  • Achievement completion rates and review scores.
  • Purchase history and wishlist items.
  • Behavioral data from users with similar gaming profiles.
🤖 Machine Learning Type

Unsupervised Learning (Collaborative Filtering / Clustering).

🔮 Model Objective

Cluster similar gamer profiles and predict the likelihood of a user purchasing an unplayed game.

💡 Potential Benefit

Boosts platform sales and promotes indie game discovery by matching games with targeted niche audiences.

📌 Practical Example

A gamer has logged 120+ hours in Elden Ring and Dark Souls III. Using collaborative filtering across millions of profiles, the model discovers that 88% of similar gamers rated Lies of P highly. It automatically features this title on the user's storefront under "Recommended for You".
